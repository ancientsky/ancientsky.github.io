兒童 COVID-19 疫苗接種建議演變時間軸 Pediatric COVID-19 Vaccine Recommendation Timeline

WHO、美國 CDC／ACIP、歐盟 ECDC／EMA、台灣疾管署（2020–2026 年 9 月）。重點是「健康兒童」建議如何從普遍接種轉成風險分層。 WHO, U.S. CDC/ACIP, ECDC/EMA, and Taiwan CDC (2020–September 2026). The through-line is how advice for healthy children shifted from universal vaccination to risk-based policy.

2020–2021｜授權下探、青少年先打 2020–2021｜Authorization moves down in age; adolescents first

2020.10–2021.07
WHO

SAGE 優先接種路線圖把疫苗留給最高風險成人、醫護。健康兒童不在優先名單。

The SAGE prioritization roadmap reserved doses for highest-risk adults and health workers. Healthy children were not a priority group.

2021.05.10
FDA / CDC

FDA 授權 Pfizer 用於 12–15 歲；隨後 CDC 建議 12 歲以上接種。

FDA authorized Pfizer for ages 12–15; CDC then recommended vaccination from age 12.

2021.05–07
EMA / ECDC

EMA 核准 Comirnaty 用於 12 歲以上。歐盟國家陸續建議全體 12–17 歲接種。

EMA approved Comirnaty from age 12. EU countries progressively recommended vaccination for all 12–17-year-olds.

2021.09.22
Taiwan CDC

校園開打 BNT，對象為 12–17 歲學生，是台灣兒童青少年新冠疫苗的起點。

School-based BNT rollout began for students aged 12–17 — Taiwan’s starting point for pediatric COVID-19 vaccination.

2021.10.29–11.02
FDA / ACIP / CDC

FDA EUA：Pfizer 5–11 歲（10 μg×2）。11/2 ACIP 建議全體 5–11 歲接種，CDC 署長採納。

FDA EUA for Pfizer in ages 5–11 (10 μg × 2). On 2 Nov, ACIP recommended it for all 5–11-year-olds; the CDC director adopted the vote.

2021.11.25
EMA

建議核准 Comirnaty 用於 5–11 歲。多數歐盟國家隨後建議全體 5–11 歲；芬蘭、德國、瑞典初期偏風險兒童。

EMA recommended Comirnaty for ages 5–11. Most EU countries then advised all children in that band; Finland, Germany and Sweden initially favored children with risk factors.

2022｜下探到 6 個月，Omicron 兒童住院上升 2022｜Age floor drops to 6 months amid Omicron pediatric burden

2022.01.21
WHO

更新優先路線圖：健康兒童與青少年列最低優先；有共病者中優先；免疫低下最高優先。強調先打完高風險成人。

Updated roadmap: healthy children and adolescents = lowest priority; those with comorbidities = medium; immunocompromised = highest. Finish high-risk adults first.

2022.05.02 / 05.25
Taiwan CDC

5/2 起 6–11 歲可打半劑莫德納；5/25 起 5–11 歲 BNT 兒童劑型全台開打（校園／院所）。背景是 BA.2 兒童腦炎重症。

From 2 May, ages 6–11 could receive half-dose Moderna; from 25 May, pediatric BNT for 5–11 rolled out nationwide. Context: BA.2 pediatric encephalitis and severe disease.

2022.06.17–18
FDA / ACIP

FDA 授權 Moderna（6 個月–5 歲，2 劑）與 Pfizer（6 個月–4 歲，3 劑）。ACIP 隨即建議全體 6 個月以上兒童接種。

FDA authorized Moderna (6 months–5 years, 2 doses) and Pfizer (6 months–4 years, 3 doses). ACIP then recommended vaccination for all children 6 months and older.

2022.06.27 / 07.21
Taiwan CDC

ACIP 開放滿 6 個月–未滿 6 歲打莫德納 2 劑。7/21 全國開打。8 月食藥署另核准 BNT 6 個月–4 歲（3 劑）。

ACIP opened Moderna (2 doses) to ages 6 months–under 6. National rollout 21 July. TFDA later authorized Pfizer for 6 months–4 years (3 doses) in August.

2022.08–10
WHO

產品建議更新：6 個月以上可用 mRNA，但健康兒童仍屬最低優先，須先覆蓋高優先族群。

Product guidance updated: mRNA vaccines usable from 6 months, but healthy children remained lowest priority until higher-priority groups were covered.

EMA / ECDC

EMA 擴適應症至 6 個月。約 7 國建議全體幼兒初種、約 13 國只建議有風險因子幼兒、部分國家不建議健康幼兒。

EMA extended indications to 6 months. About 7 countries advised primary series for all young children; ~13 only for those with risk factors; some did not advise healthy toddlers.

2023｜PHEIC 結束，健康兒童降為低優先 2023｜PHEIC ends; healthy children become low priority

2023.03.28
WHO SAGE

正式三分層：高優先（含免疫低下兒童）、中優先（有共病兒少）、低優先（健康 6 個月–17 歲）。健康兒童基礎劑「可依國情考慮」，不再是全球常規優先項目。

Three-tier roadmap: high priority (including immunocompromised children); medium (children with comorbidities); low (healthy 6 months–17 years). A primary series for healthy children “may be considered” by country context — no longer a global routine priority.

2023.05.05
WHO

COVID-19 不再構成 PHEIC。疫苗政策轉向長期風險管理。

COVID-19 was no longer a PHEIC. Vaccine policy shifted to long-term risk management.

2023.07+
EU majority

西班牙等國自 2023 年 7 月起，6 個月–12 歲健康兒童不再常規接種，改限高風險、機構住民、免疫低下同住者。ECDC 秋冬建議優先長者與脆弱族群。

From July 2023, Spain and others stopped routine vaccination of healthy children 6 months–12 years, limiting it to high-risk groups, institutional residents, and household contacts of the severely immunocompromised. ECDC autumn advice prioritized older and vulnerable people.

2023.09–11
U.S. CDC

ACIP 仍建議 6 個月以上所有人接種更新的 2023–24 配方（XBB.1.5）。美國此時仍是「全體兒童每年一劑」路線。

ACIP still recommended the updated 2023–24 (XBB.1.5) vaccine for everyone ≥6 months. The U.S. was still on a “one annual dose for all children” path.

WHO 11.10

再次修訂路線圖：健康兒童基礎劑僅依疾病負擔、成本效益、機會成本考慮。

Roadmap revised again: a primary series in healthy children only if justified by local burden, cost-effectiveness and opportunity cost.

2024｜美國仍全體建議；歐盟／WHO 已風險導向 2024｜U.S. still universal; EU and WHO already risk-based

2024.06.27
ACIP

建議所有 ≥6 個月接種 2024–25 配方（JN.1／KP.2）。健康兒童仍在常規時程表上。

Recommended 2024–25 vaccine (JN.1/KP.2) for everyone ≥6 months. Healthy children remained on the routine schedule.

2024 autumn
Typical EU

多數國家：65+、醫療高風險（含兒童）、孕婦、長照。健康兒童不再全國普打。

Most countries: ages 65+, medical high-risk groups (including children), pregnancy, long-term care. Healthy children were no longer in mass campaigns.

Taiwan CDC

秋冬公費仍包含滿 6 個月–未滿 6 歲幼兒，以及高風險兒童；6–18 歲健康者通常不在核心公費名單，但常有「尚未接種本季者可打」的擴大期。

Funded autumn program still included children 6 months–under 6 and high-risk children. Healthy 6–18-year-olds were usually outside the core list, with periodic expansions for those not yet vaccinated that season.

2025｜美國從「全體建議」改成「醫病共享決策」 2025｜U.S. shifts from universal recommendation to shared decision-making

2025.01
CDC

2025 兒童時程表仍寫「1 劑以上 2024–25 疫苗」。此時官方立場仍是普遍接種。

The 2025 child schedule still said “1 or more doses of 2024–25 vaccine.” Official policy was still universal.

2025.05.27–30
HHS / CDC

HHS 宣布不再建議健康兒童與孕婦接種新冠疫苗。CDC 兒童時程改為：健康 6 個月–17 歲改「共享臨床決策（SCDM）」；中重度免疫低下兒童仍建議接種。

HHS said COVID-19 vaccines would no longer be recommended for healthy children and pregnant people. CDC changed healthy ages 6 months–17 years to shared clinical decision-making (SCDM); moderate-to-severe immunocompromise remained a recommendation.

2025.06.12 / 10.01
Taiwan CDC

ACIP 明確轉向「風險族群導向」。114 年秋冬第一階段公費含「滿 6 個月至未滿 6 歲幼兒」（不限從未接種），高風險兒童另列。健康 6–18 歲非公費主體。

ACIP explicitly shifted to a risk-group strategy. The 2025 autumn funded list still included all children 6 months–under 6 (not only never-vaccinated), plus high-risk children. Healthy 6–18-year-olds were not the core funded group.

2025.09.18–19
ACIP

投票把新冠疫苗從「全體建議」改為個人決策／SCDM。健康兒童不再是 routine recommendation。

Voted to change COVID-19 vaccination from a universal recommendation to individual / shared decision-making. Healthy children were no longer a routine recommendation.

2026｜WHO 出正式 position paper；台灣再收窄幼兒公費 2026｜WHO position paper; Taiwan narrows funded toddlers again

2026.03
WHO SAGE

後疫情常規架構底定：高風險族群每年至少 1 劑；健康兒童與青少年不常規再接種。唯一健康兒童例外：從未接種的 6–23 個月，且該國須有「顯著疾病負擔」。

Post-pandemic routine framework: high-risk groups at least one dose a year; no routine revaccination of healthy children and adolescents. Sole healthy-child exception: previously unvaccinated 6–23-month-olds in countries with documented significant burden.

2026.06.24 / 10.01
Taiwan CDC

幼兒公費改為「從未接種之滿 6 個月–未滿 6 歲」。未滿 5 歲從未打過打 2 劑 XFG Moderna；5 歲未滿 6 歲 1 劑。健康 6–18 歲仍非公費。高風險兒童維持公費。

Toddler funding narrowed to “never vaccinated, 6 months–under 6.” Never-vaccinated children under 5: two XFG Moderna doses; ages 5–under 6: one dose. Healthy 6–18 still unfunded. High-risk children remain funded.

2026.07.31
WHO

正式《COVID-19 vaccines position paper》（WER 101:30）。寫明：健康兒少不是重症高風險；疫情後替他們接種公共衛生影響有限；不常規建議再接種。

Formal COVID-19 vaccines position paper (WER 101:30): healthy children and adolescents are not at high risk of severe disease; vaccinating them post-pandemic has limited public-health impact; revaccination is not routinely recommended.

2026.09
CDC / AAP

2026–27：18 歲以上建議接種；6 個月–17 歲中重度免疫低下建議接種；其餘兒童為家長與醫師共享決策。AAP 另行建議全體 6–23 個月，以及特定風險／從未接種的 2–18 歲。

2026–27: recommended for adults ≥18 and for immunocompromised children 6 months–17 years; other children via shared decision-making. Separately, AAP recommends all 6–23-month-olds and selected 2–18-year-olds (high risk, congregate settings, never vaccinated, or high-risk household contacts).

ECDC / EU

持續風險導向：兒童只在有重症危險因子時建議年度接種。ECDC 不發單一「全歐兒童必打」時程，由各國衛生部決定。

Still risk-based: annual pediatric doses mainly for children with severe-disease risk factors. ECDC does not issue a single binding EU child schedule; ministries of health decide.
